# AdSense Content-Quality Baseline Assessment — noorapp.in

Date: 2026-09-28 (IST)
Production: https://noorapp.in · commit `808c9f0`
Scope: BASELINE of the CURRENT production state. Evidence-based only; no traffic,
revenue, or ranking data is claimed (none measured).

Evidence sources: live spot-checks of homepage, /quran/112, /hadith/sahih-bukhari/bangla/chapter-1,
/dua/dua-for-parents, /quiz, /sources, /about (fetched 2026-09-28), plus repo reports
`NOOR_ADSENSE_REMEDIATION_FINAL_2026-09-27.md` and `NOOR_EVIDENCE_FIRST_REVIEW_FINAL_2026-09-28.md`.

## 1. Genuine user value — ADEQUATE

The site delivers real, working tools for daily religious practice in three languages:
Quran reader with Arabic + Bangla/English/Urdu translations, full Sahih al-Bukhari
(Arabic + translation with per-hadith references, verified live on chapter-1),
218 duas, 74 stories, a 281-question quiz, prayer times and Qibla — free, no subscription.
However, 211 surah/chapter pages currently carry no introductory editorial content,
so a meaningful portion of the indexed surface serves only raw text + metadata.

## 2. Original editorial value — WEAK

The core corpus is faithfully republished sourced texts (translations via AlQuran Cloud API,
hadith records) with honest labeling but a thin original layer: 211 introduction slots are
empty, quiz explanations have a median of ~120 characters, and 33 of 74 stories are under
800 characters (kept short deliberately to avoid fabrication). The editorial contribution —
navigation, attribution, honest uncertainty markers — is real but not yet substantive
enough to constitute significant original editorial value at the site's scale (973 URLs).

## 3. Source transparency — STRONG

The strongest area. /sources names every source explicitly: Uthmani Mus-haf (Madinah script),
AlQuran Cloud API, translators by name (Muhiuddin Khan — Bengali, Saheeh International —
English, Ahmed Ali — Urdu), Imam Bukhari with death dates, Kutub as-Sittah roadmap, and
grading authorities (Ibn Hajar, al-Albani). Chapter pages carry per-hadith cross-references;
where translator data is genuinely absent it is disclosed honestly rather than invented.
An editorial-review process and correction path are documented.

## 4. Content depth — WEAK (substance, not word count)

Quran and hadith pages are appropriately complete (full scripture + translation is not
"thin"). The weakness is concentrated in the editorial layers: 281 indexed quiz pages
with ~120-char explanations, 33 short stories, and 211 surah/chapter pages with no
introduction — a substantial share of the 973-URL index where the page offers little
beyond raw data or a one-line answer. No keyword stuffing or generic SEO paragraphs
exist anywhere, which is to the site's credit.

## 5. Duplicate content — WEAK (mitigated, residual)

The 30 exact-duplicate quiz pairs are render-layer canonicalized to primaries (sitemap
clean, primaries self-canonical, mapping re-generated byte-identical with no drift —
verified 2026-09-28). Residual: ~10 near-duplicate pairs plus 6 unmapped near-duplicates
(visible in the live quiz hub: Ar-Rahman ×3, Al-Malik ×3, Ayatul Kursi pair, Takbir pair,
tawaf questions ×3) remain indexed without consolidation or documented differentiation.
Cross-language (bn/en) question variants are legitimate; the same-language near-duplicates
are the active pattern.

## 6. Programmatic thin pages — ADEQUATE

No mass-generated placeholder or SEO-spam pattern. Quality gates (12/12 PASS) confirm:
973 sitemap URLs all return 200, 973 unique titles, 8/8 invalid routes fail closed
(404/410 + noindex), no placeholder-leak tokens, no ad code in prerender output. Pages
are data-driven templates, but each carries unique substantive content (verse text,
hadith text, dua text, question text) — programmatic in delivery, not in content.

## 7. Internal linking — STRONG

Every surah page links prev/next surah (named) + All Surahs + back (verified in report);
all 97 chapter pages have boundary-aware prev/next chapter navigation; the quiz hub
links all 281 questions grouped by 19 categories (verified live); the story hub links
74/74; dua detail pages carry same-category related links on all 218; the Bukhari hub
links /sources. No orphaned indexed content found.

## 8. Indexation quality — STRONG

973/973 sitemap URLs return HTTP 200 with `index,follow`; all self-canonical; invalid
Quran/Dua/Story/Hadith/Quiz/arbitrary routes return 404/410 with `noindex,follow`
(verified by gates and spot-check: a non-existent dua slug returned 404 upstream).
robots.txt serves correctly. No index bloat, no soft-404s.

## 9. Trust signals — STRONG

Homepage links About, Support & Feedback, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Use; /about
states mission, editorial approach, and an explicit error-reporting invitation;
/sources documents the full source chain and correction path. The homepage frames the
site as "a digital learning tool, not a substitute for a qualified scholar." No ads,
no monetization code, no deceptive elements. A complete trust-signal set for a site
of this kind.

## 10. Religious-source integrity — ADEQUATE

No invented Islamic content, citations, scholar names, grades, or translations anywhere
(verified across three audit phases). Translator attributions are rendered (Muhiuddin
Khan in bot HTML) and honest disclaimers cover genuinely missing data. Render-layer
suppression of the 160 templated dua virtue claims is active (verified live). However,
the underlying data layer is still compromised: 160 dua rows retain templated virtue
claims (RLS-blocked cleanup), 17 quiz records carry question/answer-key defects
(e.g. wrong prophet count, false premise) that remain indexed, and dua explanation
prose still contains virtue-adjacent editorial language ("/dua/dua-for-parents" states
'এই দোয়ার ফজিলত' অপরিসীম). Public surface: clean and honest. Data layer: not yet.

## Overall baseline verdict: NOT YET READY

The technical and trust foundations are genuinely strong (indexation, linking,
transparency, trust signals all STRONG; no programmatic spam; no ads). The HIGH-risk
templated-virtue signal is eliminated from the public surface. But an AdSense reviewer
evaluates content substance, and three active gaps remain at scale: (a) thin editorial
depth across ~590 indexed pages (281 quiz pages with ~120-char explanations, 33 short
stories, 211 intro-less surah/chapter pages); (b) residual same-language near-duplicate
quiz clusters; (c) unresolved data-layer integrity issues (160 dua rows with templated
claims, 17 quiz records with question/key defects). Keep the AdSense review paused
until the remediation workflow produces verified, evidence-backed improvements in
these three areas. No approval is claimed or guaranteed.
